# Technical Manifest: Manifold Composer Architecture

## 1. B-Spline Surface Manifolds
Every geometric "puzzle piece" is a 2D surface $S(u, v)$ parameterized by $(u, v) \in [0, 1]^2$. 
The surface is generated by a KAN with topology $(2, 32, 32, 3+K)$, where the output layer is an **Explicit SoA Spline Layer**.

The output dimensions are partitioned into:
*   **Geometric Channels (3):** $(X, Y, Z)$ coordinates defining the physical manifold.
*   **Semantic Channels (K):** Confidence heatmaps $C_1, C_2, \dots, C_k \in [0, 1]$ representing:
    *   **Rigid Anchors:** High-probability zones for buttons, rivets, or bolts.
    *   **Joint Anchors:** Zones for flexible attachments like straps or pockets.
    *   **Structural Sockets:** Locations where child manifolds (branches, limbs) are allowed to sprout.

## 2. Boundary Pinning & Sockets
The "Socket" is a 1D spline boundary $B(t)$. To join Piece A to Piece B:
*   The **Parent Socket** (e.g., $v=1$) of Piece A outputs a 1D spline.
*   The **Child Socket** (e.g., $v=0$) of Piece B is **Pinned** to the parent spline.
*   **Mathematical Enforcement:** The first row of B-spline coefficients in Piece B is calculated such that $S(u, 0) = B(u)$.

## 3. Symbolic Graph Orchestration
We separate the "Organic Neural Math" from the "Logical Structural Symbolic" reasoning.
*   **The Orchestrator:** Maintains a **Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG)** of KAN instances.
*   **The LLM Solver:** Reasons about topological requirements (e.g., "Add three more arms to the torso").
*   **The Pipeline:**
    1.  LLM generates JSON DAG.
    2.  Orchestrator allocates KAN nodes and pins their sockets.
    3.  Zig Kernel evaluates the whole graph at AVX-512 speeds.
    4.  Vulkan Shader renders the continuous manifold.

## 4. Continuity Targets
*   **$C^0$ (Positional):** No gaps. The surface is watertight.
*   **$C^1$ (Tangency):** Smooth curvature. No sharp edges at the seams.
*   **$C^2$ (Curvature):** High-end geometric continuity for aerodynamic/industrial modeling.
